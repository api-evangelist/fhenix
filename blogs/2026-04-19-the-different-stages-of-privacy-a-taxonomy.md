Not all privacy is equal. This post introduces a four-stage taxonomy for evaluating onchain privacy systems - from hardware-dependent TEE confidentiality (Stage 0) to cryptographic threshold schemes (Stages 1–2) to the theoretical ceiling of indistinguishability obfuscation (Stage Aleph). A practical framework for distinguishing real privacy guarantees from marketing language.
