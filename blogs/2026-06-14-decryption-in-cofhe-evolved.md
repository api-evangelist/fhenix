CoFHE makes encrypted computation practical for on-chain applications — but the sharp edge, especially for new builders, has always been decryption: when you can decrypt, who can decrypt, and what it means to “reveal” a value in a public system. With the new @cofhe/sdk, we’re shipping a clearer, safer decryption model that matches how real applications behave.
