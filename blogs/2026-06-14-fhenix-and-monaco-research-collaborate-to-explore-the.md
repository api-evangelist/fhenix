Fhenix and Monaco Research are excited to announce a research collaboration to investigate how fully homomorphic encryption (FHE) can bring meaningful confidentiality to onchain trading infrastructure. Together, these teams are exploring the design space around dark pools, encrypted request-for-quote (RFQ) systems, and the broader challenge of enabling private execution in decentralized markets, without sacrificing the trust guarantees and liquidity that make DeFi compelling.
