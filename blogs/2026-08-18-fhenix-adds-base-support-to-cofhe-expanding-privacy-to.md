At Fhenix, our mission has always been simple: bring encryption to Ethereum and make privacy usable. Today, we’re taking another major step - [CoFHE] (https://www.fhenix.io/blog/introducing-cofhe-fhe-based-encryption-everywhere-with-a-single-line-of-code), our Fully Homomorphic Encryption coprocessor, is now live on Base Sepolia. This marks the first FHE coprocessor running on Base Sepolia, unlocking encrypted computation and private smart contracts directly on one of Ethereum’s most active L2s.
